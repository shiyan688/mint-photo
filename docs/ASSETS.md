生成方式：内置 imagegen；2026-10-24。生成提示：

> An original editorial lifestyle portrait of a fictional adult East Asian woman in her late twenties, shoulder-length natural dark brown hair, cream knit sweater, seated beside a sunlit window in a modern cafe. Natural skin texture, realistic pores, relaxed smile, soft afternoon side light, muted warm wood and sage interior, half-body 4:5 composition. No text, watermark, logo, frame or interface; not a celebrity.
